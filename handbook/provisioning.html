We will be talking about provisioning in the computing sense and mostly related to just high performance computing. Although this can go pretty far as HPC spans most of computing.

Definition:

Bring some resource online for use in the HPC Cluster such as:

  • Nodes
  • Users
  • Virtual Machines
  • Storage
