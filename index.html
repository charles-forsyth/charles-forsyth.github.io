From the Deckplates to the Data Center

My journey began troubleshooting radar systems on the USS Lake Champlain. That discipline—understanding the physics of the machine—now drives my work architecting petabyte-scale cloud infrastructure for the University of California.

I don't just write code; I design systems that build themselves. As the pioneer of Directed Agentic Engineering (DAE), I direct squads of AI agents to solve enterprise-scale problems.

In between came two decades in the machine room: running research clusters at Corning Incorporated, serving as an XSEDE and ACCESS Campus Champion at UCR, and serving as Vice Chair and then Chair of the UC Research IT Committee across the University of California system. Curriculum vitae

Flagship Innovations

ursa-bifrost: AI Meets the HPC Cluster

Let any AI assistant work a Slurm cluster, safely. One Go binary that is both an MCP server and a CLI. Claude, Gemini, Codex and Hermes get typed, audited, read-mostly access to jobs, failure diagnosis, costs and software, with Google sign-in, tiered permissions and two-step approval for anything that spends money.

A Family of MCP Servers for Research

AI assistants, connected safely to real systems. Beyond ursa-bifrost: nrp-mcp runs researchers' work on the National Research Platform's Nautilus GPU cluster from plain language, with NRP policy built in and nothing running until the person says yes. Internal servers cover the Nexus knowledge graph, the AI Gateway's labs and keys, the campus directory, data security plan cases and the Google Cloud fleet audit. Each one has sign-in, roles and an audit trail.

Ursa Major HPC Cluster on Google Cloud

A cluster that costs nothing when idle. An elastic Slurm cluster built with Google Cloud's Cluster Toolkit: CPU, high-memory and NVIDIA L4 GPU partitions that grow on demand and shut down when the queue empties, a curated scientific software stack, per-project limits, and ursa-bifrost so AI assistants can submit and diagnose jobs.

Internal service

UCR AI Gateway

One governed door to frontier AI. A single OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible endpoint to Gemini, Claude, open-weight and National Research Platform models, with per-lab budgets, spend limits and a full audit log, plus an admin MCP server for labs, keys and usage.

Internal service

Nexus: The Research Knowledge Graph

The system of record for Research Computing. A Postgres and pgvector graph of researchers, labs, cloud projects, funding and every interaction, run through a CLI and an OAuth MCP server with role-gated tools, so AI assistants can work the graph with a full audit trail.
