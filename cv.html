Summary

Research computing leader in high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence/machine learning (AI/ML) with more than 25 years of experience in system administration, software development and research IT. As Director of Research Computing at the University of California, Riverside, I lead the design, implementation and optimization of the HPC systems and applications that drive research across UCR's disciplines, including AI and ML, along with the training programs and research support that build the skills of our faculty and students.

My career spans R1 universities, a Fortune 500 research campus, systemwide University of California leadership and United States Navy electronics. My expertise covers HPC system administration, AI/ML integration, cloud research computing, software development and documentation, license management and systems design.
